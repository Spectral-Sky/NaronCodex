LAND & PLANET GUIDE
Each AW planet has a land type that determines which tools work best on it. Your daily featured land is picked from your owned lands with an Alien Legends building. Mine on that land in AW today to increase your Mine Mult bonus. Crystite is always the rarest and most valuable mineral — land types that boost Manipulators tend to produce the highest scores.
MAGOR VOLCANIC
⛏ Extractor +25%
💥 Explosive +15%
🦾 ExoTool +0%
🔬 Manipulator−15%
Harsh volcanic mountains — constant tectonic activity pushes dense ore seams to the surface. Extractors and Explosives are built for this terrain. Deep crystite work is impractical in the heat.
EYEKE BIOSPHERE
💥 Explosive +30%
⛏ Extractor +10%
🦾 ExoTool −5%
🔬 Manipulator−10%
Vast oceans with lush coastal grasslands — enormous biomass accumulation across the surface layer. Explosives clear the most organic material per detonation anywhere in the system.

Towering icy mountain ranges — silica and crystite locked deep in permafrost and glacial rock. Mechanical drills seize in the cold. Precision tools and chemical dissolution are the only practical approaches.

How to Play — World Mine
01
Pick Your Tools
Select 1–3 AW mining tools from your wallet and lock in your loadout. Each tool mines independently. Your loadout is locked for 24 hours — you can't swap until tomorrow.
02
Daily Extraction Drop
When you lock in, your tools immediately drop today's materials. Watch the card reveal — each tool flips to show what it mined. Materials accumulate in your weekly totals.
03
Land Type Synergy
Each day features a different land type from a rotating planet. Match your tool type to the land for a score bonus — a Manipulator on Veles (Oceanic) massively outscores one on Magor (Volcanic).
04
Shine = Score
The single biggest lever is your tool's shine tier. Common Stardust ≈ Rare Gold ≈ Epic Stone in score output. Legendary and Mythic tools start at ×30 even on Stone. Invest in shine.
05
Daily Rarity Pool
Each day boosts one rarity tier — Common Day multiplies Common tools by ×8. On Rare Day your Rare Stardust tools pull away from the pack. Check the season banner before locking in.
06
Perks & Combos (coming soon)
Save your elemental mana to unlock weekly perks. Play 6 AW crew NFTs as an elemental hand — 3+3, 2+2+2, or prismatic 1-of-each — to fire combo effects that multiply your score for days.
