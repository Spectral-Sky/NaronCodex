◈ Mission Control Adventures

← Naron Codex

Every Mission Control adventure a wallet has sent NFTs on and not yet claimed, read straight from the adventure.mc contract. The NFTs wait there until the wallet that sent them claims the adventure.

Claiming signs adventure.mc::claimadv as the wallet that sent the NFTs; the contract sends them back to that same wallet once the adventure's return time has passed. Only that wallet can claim. For a wallet that lived in Wombat, import its private key into Anchor and sign in with Anchor here.
