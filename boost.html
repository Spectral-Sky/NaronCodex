Connect your wallet to load your lands.
PAYOUT / DAY is each land's rating payout, paid once a day around 10:36 UTC: rating ÷ 1,000,000 × the current rate per million rating points. Land commission is paid per wallet and can't be split by land: its daily figure is your unclaimed commission divided by the days since your last commission claim (or, right after a claim, that claim divided by the gap before it). Your own mining rewards are not included. Untick INCLUDE on a land to leave it out of both buttons. Unlocking slot #2–15 costs 160 · 260 · 420 · 680 · 1,100 · 1,800 · 2,800 · 4,600 · 7,400 · 12,000 · 18,000 · 30,000 · 50,000 · 80,000 TLM. Each boost is a TLM transfer to boost.worlds plus awlndratings::boost, the same as the official site. A boost multiplies rating by 4 TLM +0.03% · 8 TLM +0.05% · 16 TLM +0.08% · 32 TLM +0.13% · 64 TLM +0.21%. The boost that reaches max rating is charged in full but only lifts the land to the cap, and a land at max rejects boosts, so those lands are excluded and no land gets boosts past the cap. Slots reset at 00:00 UTC. Days to max assume every slot is used at the selected level.
