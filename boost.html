Connect your wallet to load your lands.
Each boost is a TLM transfer to boost.worlds plus awlndratings::boost, the same as the official site. A boost multiplies rating by 4 TLM +0.03% · 8 TLM +0.05% · 16 TLM +0.08% · 32 TLM +0.13% · 64 TLM +0.21%. The boost that reaches max rating is charged in full but only lifts the land to the cap, and a land at max rejects boosts, so those lands are excluded and no land gets boosts past the cap. Slots reset at 00:00 UTC. Days to max assume every slot is used at the selected level.
