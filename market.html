Prices are in gems, exactly as market.ale records them. WAX values are an estimate only, converted at the large gem pack rate (4,999 WAX for 12,500 gems) — the game sells gems in packs, so there is no true market rate. Sellers receive the price minus a 15% processing fee (minimum 1 gem). Auctions run 48 hours; a bid must beat the current one by 2 gems or 10%, whichever is larger. Listings are read live from the chain; sale history comes from a sheet that rebuilds every sale from chain history, so nothing is missed.
